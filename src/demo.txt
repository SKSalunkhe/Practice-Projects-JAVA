
My School

My school is one of the most important and favorite places in my life. It is not just a place where I study books, but a second home where I learn, grow, and make happy memories.

The Building and Campus

My school building is large, bright, and very clean. It has spacious, well-ventilated classrooms with comfortable desks and smartboards. The campus also features a big library filled with interesting storybooks and reference materials, a computer lab, and science laboratories. Outside, there is a huge, green playground where we play games and sports during our break time.

Our Teachers
Our teachers are very kind, patient, and hardworking. They do not just teach us lessons from textbooks; they explain things simply, encourage us to ask questions, and guide us to be disciplined and honest. They always support us whenever we find a subject difficult.

Activities and Friends

I have many wonderful friends at school. We sit together, share our tiffin, study as a team, and play games on the field. Apart from academics, our school encourages us to take part in drawing, music, dance, debates, and sports events.

Conclusion

I love my school very much. It teaches me good values, kindness, and teamwork every single day, and I feel proud to be a student there.
